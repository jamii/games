Passives that apply to adjacent units too?

* Is Stun way too good? Should stun be something like add N tokens, where one token is removed per turn and the power is disabled until all tokens are removed?
* Misc power ideas
    * Spooky Action at a Distance: Pasive. like Heavy, but only at exactly range 3 
    * Sturdy: Passive. This unit cannot be moved by enemy powers
    * Inviolable: Passive. Takes 2 slots. This unit is immune to enemy powers [should this take 3 slots?]
    * Screen: Passive. Takes 2 slots. Adjacent enemy units do not count for scoring
    * Splash: Passive. When this unit targets a single unit, it can also target an unit adjacent to the target [non-recursive]
    * Surveyor: Active. Remove one of this unit's powers. Draw a power, then discard a power from your hand.

* Movement ideas
    * Strafe: Active. Move to an empty adjacent space that is not directly in front of or behind you
    * Opportunistic: Passive. When an adjacent friendly unit moves itself exactly one space, this unit may move one space in the same direction. Could limit to once per turn and also prevent Opportunistic from triggering Opportunistic
    * Goalie: Active. Move adjacent target unit to an adjacent empty space farther from the Flag
    * Rally: Active. Move adjacent target unit to an adjacent empty space closer to the Flag
    * Recoil: Active. Move one space. If you did, move a previously adjacent unit one space away from your previous space
    * Needy: Passive. Whenever an adjacent friendly unit moves or is moved, move this unit into that space
    * Flanker: Active. If on an edge space, move to any empty edge space that is not a home space    
    * Plow: Active. Takes 2 slots. Move into a space that has a unit, pushing that unit into another space
    * Dogpile: modify to take 2 slots
